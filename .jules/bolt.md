## 2024-05-15 - Optimizing bulk contact saves in DRF serializers
**Learning:** By default, DRF's `ListSerializer` iterates over instances and calls `save()` individually, leading to N+1 `update_or_create` queries. In PostgreSQL 9.5+, Django supports `bulk_create` with `update_conflicts=True`, allowing us to batch insert/update models efficiently. To fix this, we override the `ListSerializer.create` method and map the deserialized array to model instances, executing a single bulk query while passing `unique_fields` and `update_fields` appropriately.
**Action:** Always check `many=True` endpoints and standard array iterations for N+1 update bottlenecks. In Django+Postgres, prefer a single `bulk_create` call with `update_conflicts=True` in a custom `ListSerializer` over `update_or_create` within loops. Ensure you correctly instantiate models via `Model(**item)` before passing them to `bulk_create`.
