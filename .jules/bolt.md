## 2026-04-26 - Optimize reply_whatsapp with select_related
**Learning:** In Django, iterating over a queryset (like `contactosxcampa`) and accessing related fields (like `c.campania` and `c.campania.estado`) within a list comprehension triggers lazy loading and results in an N+1 query problem. This can significantly degrade performance when processing numerous rows, especially in real-time webhook endpoints like `reply_whatsapp`.
**Action:** When filtering a queryset that will be iterated over and related fields accessed, always eagerly fetch the necessary relationships using `.select_related()` (for `ForeignKey` or `OneToOneField`) or `.prefetch_related()` (for `ManyToManyField` or `ReverseForeignKey`) in the initial query. In this case, appending `.select_related('campania', 'campania__estado')` reduced 22 unoptimized queries down to just 2 optimized queries for 10 records.
