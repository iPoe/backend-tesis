## 2024-11-01 - Optimizing Background Tasks and Resolving N+1 Queries
**Learning:** Celery tasks such as `llamar_usuarias`, `envMensajeUsuarias`, `enviar_correos`, and `enviarWhatsapp` in `campaigns/tasks.py` had severe N+1 query and redundant lookup issues due to fetching related ForeignKey models sequentially or re-fetching objects already available by ID. The original code in `llamar_usuarias` also had a bug where it re-queried `Campania` and then looped over an undefined variable `numerosUsuarias`.
**Action:** Bypassed redundant `.get()` queries for foreign keys by assigning IDs directly (`campania_id_id=ID`, `medio_id_id=mId`, `contacto_cc_id=obj.contacto_id`). Added `.select_related('contacto')` to prevent N+1 queries. Used `bulk_create()` to avoid looping and calling `.save()` on every iteration (e.g., in `enviarWhatsapp`). Replaced fetching models just to update a state (like `Campania.objects.get` then `camp.estado = inactiva` and `camp.save()`) with `Campania.objects.filter(pk=campID).update(estado_id=3)`. Deduplicated outcome variables to optimize DB hits.
